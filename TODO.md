## Land-search product gaps

- [ ] Add listing-discovery filters: price range, price per acre, property type/use, beds/baths, listing status, days on market, keywords, and richer acreage ranges.
- [ ] Add accounts or durable user profiles with saved searches, favorites/shortlists, named and shareable searches, property comparison lists, and new-listing, price-change, and status-change alerts.
- [ ] Add a synchronized, sortable, paginated listing-results view with cards—not just map filtering.
- [ ] Add side-by-side parcel comparison and a land feasibility scorecard: slope/buildable area, frontage/access, utilities, water/well risk, flood/fire exposure, zoning constraints, taxes, and price per acre.
- [ ] Integrate or link more directly to due-diligence data: owner/title and encumbrance context, annual taxes and assessed value, easements and verified access, utilities, septic suitability, permits, building restrictions, mineral rights, and water rights.
- [ ] Improve access and infrastructure analysis: routable travel/access, legal versus physical access, road type and maintenance, driveway distance, utility proximity, and travel time to towns and essential services.
- [ ] Add market intelligence: comparable-sales search, price and days-on-market trends, price-change history, and area market summaries.
- [ ] Improve search ergonomics: autocomplete/typeahead, fuzzy matching, search within current map bounds, and use a drawn area to filter listings.
- [ ] Add richer listing content: photo gallery, documents/disclosures/surveys, video, broker contact flow, and clear per-listing data freshness and status timestamps.
- [ ] Add collaboration and export: shared collections, team/family comments, printable property reports, and export selected results as CSV, PDF, and GeoJSON.
