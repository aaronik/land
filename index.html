How to use the map

Computer

  • Move: click and drag
  • Zoom: use the mouse wheel, trackpad, or + / − buttons
  • Rotate: right-click and drag, or hold Ctrl while dragging
  • Tilt: right-click and drag up or down
  • Inspect: click a parcel, listing, spring, well, or geology area

Phone or tablet

  • Move: drag with one finger
  • Zoom: pinch with two fingers
  • Rotate: twist with two fingers
  • Tilt: drag up or down with two fingers
  • Your location: press the location button and allow permission

Use the 3D button for terrain, or the ruler button to measure distance. Press the compass to return north-up.

County owner & assessment lookup

Siskiyou County directs public parcel, value, and map lookups to ParcelQuest Lite. It is an external service; information shown there is not copied into this map.

The APN will be copied and the County’s lookup will open in a new tab.
